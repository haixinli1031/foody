    How this works

    We read the ingredient list from the label (via the Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Food Facts databases, or from a photo you confirm), match each name against a reference list of substances, and show you what named authorities say about each one.

    Evidence tiers

    • A Regulatory list in force: EU Cosmetics Regulation annexes, REACH restrictions, US FDA rules, California Proposition 65 and state laws.
    • B Authoritative scientific body: IARC monographs, ECHA harmonised classification, SCCS opinions, ECHA endocrine-disruptor assessment list.
    • C Advocacy or screening list published by an NGO. Hidden unless you turn it on. Always labelled.
    • D A fact about the label itself, such as an undisclosed fragrance mixture or a declared allergen.

    What we do not do

    We do not give products a safety score. We do not know the concentration of any ingredient, so we cannot calculate risk. We never say a product is safe, unsafe, toxic or clean. The only verdict is whether a product matches the preferences you set.

    Exposure matters

    A restriction that applies to leave-on products may not apply to a rinse-off product. Choose the product type on the result screen and the notes adjust.

    Data status

    Every entry cites its source. Reference data has not yet been reviewed by a board-certified toxicologist. Treat it as a draft and check the source before acting on it.

    Brands and corrections

    If you make a product we list and the information is out of date or wrong, open a correction request. We mark the listing under review within one day and resolve it within five business days.
